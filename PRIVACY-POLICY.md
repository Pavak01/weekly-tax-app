# Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: 20 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Weekly Tax App collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the mobile app and backend API.

## 1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to users of Weekly Tax App, including the mobile app and related API services.

## 2. Information we collect

### A. Account and sign-in information
- Email address
- Password (stored as a hash, not plain text)
- Optional two-step verification information (encrypted secret and verification state)
- Password reset metadata (hashed reset code and expiry timestamp)

### B. Tax and bookkeeping information you enter
- Tax year and weekly/monthly/daily entry dates
- Income totals
- Expense items, categories, and reimbursed amounts
- Optional company/client name field (company providing services for)

### C. Receipt information
- Receipt file content you upload (for example PDF or image)
- Receipt metadata (file name, MIME type, file size, upload time)

### D. Derived information generated by the app
- Tax estimate values (for example estimated income tax and NI)
- Summaries, breakdowns, and export views generated from your entries

### E. Device-local app state
On your device, the app stores:
- Session token in secure storage (where available)
- Basic profile/session state and convenience UI values (for example selected dates) in app storage

## 3. How we use your information

We use data to:
- Create and secure your account
- Authenticate sign-in and maintain your session
- Support optional two-step verification
- Process weekly/monthly/daily submissions
- Calculate tax estimates and compliance warnings
- Show summaries, audit history, and exports
- Store and retrieve receipt attachments linked to your entries
- Operate and protect the service (for example rate-limiting login and upload endpoints)

## 4. Where and how data is stored

### A. Database storage
Your account, entries, expenses, summaries, and receipt metadata are stored in PostgreSQL.

### B. Receipt file storage
Receipts are stored either:
- Locally on server storage, or
- In configured object storage (S3-compatible)

The app stores a receipt path reference and uses authenticated, signed download links for access.

### C. Authentication and access controls
- Protected API routes require a valid Bearer token
- Receipt downloads require both authentication and a short-lived signed token
- Passwords are bcrypt-hashed
- Two-step secrets are encrypted at rest on the server

## 5. Sharing and disclosures

We do not sell personal information.

We may share data with infrastructure/service providers that run core app infrastructure (for example hosting, database, and object storage) only as needed to provide the service.

We may disclose data if required by law or valid legal process.

## 6. Data retention

- Data remains stored until deleted by you or by service administrators according to operational requirements.
- The app supports deletion of week-level data and clearing all entries, which removes linked records and associated receipt files from configured storage paths.
- Backup copies may persist for operational recovery windows.

## 7. Security measures

We apply technical controls including:
- Password hashing
- Encrypted two-step secrets
- Authenticated API access
- Signed, time-limited receipt download tokens
- Upload restrictions (file type/size limits)
- Basic endpoint rate limiting

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to reduce risk using reasonable safeguards.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can:
- Access your submitted tax data in-app (summary/audit/export screens)
- Delete week data or clear all entries from your account
- Enable or disable two-step verification

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights (for example access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, or restriction). Contact us to request these.

## 9. Children

This service is not intended for children under 13 (or the age required by local law).

## 10. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on hosting and infrastructure providers.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes should be communicated through app updates or repository documentation updates.

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the app operator/support team and include:
- Account email
- Request type (access, correction, deletion, etc.)
- Relevant tax year or date range
