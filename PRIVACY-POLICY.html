Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Weekly Tax App collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the mobile app and backend API.

App name on Google Play: Weekly Tax App
Developer name on Google Play: Pavak01

Data deletion request (Google Play)

How to request account and data deletion:

  1. Email support@aplccommodities.com from your account email.
  2. Use subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your full name and request permanent deletion of your Weekly Tax App account and associated data.
  4. We may request identity verification from the registered account email.

Public deletion request page: ACCOUNT-DELETION.html

Processing target: within 30 days of receiving a verified request.

What we delete vs what may be retained

Deleted after a verified deletion request:

  • Account profile data
  • Weekly/monthly/daily entry records
  • Expense records
  • Receipt metadata and receipt files in active storage
  • Tax summary records linked to the account
  • Two-step verification secret and reset metadata linked to the account

May be retained for limited periods:

  • Security and abuse-prevention logs: up to 30 days
  • Backup snapshots: until backup rotation expires (typically up to 30 days)
  • Records required by applicable law: retained only as legally required

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: email address and hashed password.
  • Optional two-step verification metadata.
  • Tax and bookkeeping entries you submit (daily/weekly/monthly).
  • Expense details and reimbursement values.
  • Receipt files and metadata (name, type, size, upload timestamp).
  • Derived values (tax estimate, summaries, exports).
  • Device-local state for session and convenience settings.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate users and secure accounts.
  • Store and process entries, expenses, summaries, and exports.
  • Generate tax estimates and compliance warnings.
  • Store and retrieve receipt attachments.
  • Operate, secure, and monitor service quality.

3. Storage and security

  • Core data is stored in PostgreSQL with Point-in-Time Recovery (4-week backup window).
  • Daily automated database backups with automated archiving.
  • Receipt files stored in S3-compatible object storage with magic-byte content validation.
  • Protected API routes require authenticated bearer tokens with version-based session revocation.
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt; two-step secrets encrypted at rest.
  • Two-factor backup recovery codes: SHA-256 hashed, single-use, account-locked fallback.
  • Audit logging of authentication events, password changes, and admin actions.
  • Signed, time-limited receipt download tokens (5-minute expiry).

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by core infrastructure providers as needed to operate the service.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data is retained until deleted by user action or operational policy.
  • In-app options support clearing period data and clearing all entries.
  • Backup copies may persist for limited recovery windows (typically up to 30 days).

6. Your choices

  • View your data in summary, audit, and export screens.
  • Delete period data or clear all entry data.
  • Enable or disable two-step verification.

7. Account deletion

For full account and data deletion, use the public deletion request page: ACCOUNT-DELETION.html
